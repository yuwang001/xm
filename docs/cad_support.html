AnyCAD Support

AnyCAD is a lightweight viewer for CAD drawings, PDFs, vector files, and 3D models on iPhone and iPad.

Contact

If you need help, have feedback, or want to report a file that does not open correctly, please email us at hello@sheetmind.com.

Supported Files

AnyCAD supports a growing set of technical drawing and 3D model formats, including:

  • DXF, SVG, PDF, HPGL, and PLT
  • USDZ, USD, DAE, ABC, OBJ, STL, PLY, glTF, GLB, 3MF, XYZ, and OFF

Troubleshooting

  • Make sure the file is stored locally or available through the Files app.
  • Very large files may take longer to load, especially complex 3D models.
  • If a model appears empty, try exporting it again from the original CAD or 3D application.
  • Some advanced CAD features, proprietary extensions, or unsupported geometry may not render.

Privacy

AnyCAD opens files selected by you. The app does not require an account and does not sell personal data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
