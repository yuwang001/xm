Privacy Policy

AnyCAD Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 1, 2026

AnyCAD is designed as a local file viewer for CAD drawings, PDFs, vector files, and 3D models. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

Information We Collect

AnyCAD does not require an account and does not collect personal information from you.

Your Files

AnyCAD opens files that you choose through iOS, such as files selected from the Files app. These files are processed on your device for viewing. We do not upload your documents, drawings, PDFs, or 3D models to our servers.

Analytics and Tracking

AnyCAD does not use third-party advertising trackers and does not sell personal data.

Diagnostics

Apple may provide developers with crash reports and diagnostics if you have enabled sharing analytics with app developers in your device settings. These reports are provided by Apple and are used only to improve app reliability.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We do not share your files with third parties.

Children's Privacy

AnyCAD does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the effective date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at hello@sheetmind.com.
