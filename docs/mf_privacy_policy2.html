Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MobilePy ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

MobilePy is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your source code or files
  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Contacts or photos
  • Usage analytics or tracking data

All Python code you write and execute runs entirely on your device. Your projects and files are stored locally and are never uploaded to any server.

2. Subscription Information

MobilePy offers subscription-based access managed through Apple's App Store. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID to verify your subscription entitlements. No personally identifiable information is shared with RevenueCat.

All payment processing is handled by Apple. We do not have access to your payment details, credit card information, or Apple ID.

3. Package Installation

When you install Python packages via pip, the app connects to the Python Package Index (PyPI) to download packages. These network requests are made directly from your device and do not pass through our servers.

4. Data Storage

All your data — source code, project files, settings, and preferences — is stored locally on your device. We do not operate any servers that store user data.

5. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • RevenueCat — Subscription management. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store — Payment processing. Apple Privacy Policy

6. Children's Privacy

MobilePy does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. The app does not require account creation or the submission of any personal data.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
